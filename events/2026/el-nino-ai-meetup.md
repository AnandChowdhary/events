# How AI agents can be used in practice at El Niño

At El Niño's AI Meetup in Enschede, I gave a talk about moving AI agents toward autonomy through stages of trust: first observing, then asking for approval, then supervised tasks, and finally autonomous work.

The framing: agents = context + tools + trust.
